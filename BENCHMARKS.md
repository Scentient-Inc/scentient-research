# Benchmark goals and publication status

No numerical benchmark result is published in this release. These are evaluation goals, not completed validation claims.

| Area | Question | Public release status |
|---|---|---|
| Human grounding | Do sensory encounters add useful information about later personal experience? | Research direction; no human result released |
| Personal alignment | Does an agent interpret intent, uncertainty and corrections faithfully? | Evaluation agenda; no score released |
| Cross-model continuity | Does relevant meaning remain useful across model changes? | Research direction; no transfer result released |
| Multimodal learning | Which sensory relationships improve a defined task? | Research direction; no comparative result released |
| Embodied behavior | Can an agent use observations and consequences in controlled tasks? | Prototype previews and simulation demonstrations; no generalization claim |

## Release standard

A released result should identify the task, evidence category, evaluation setting, baseline, version, result, and limitations. Human, synthetic, simulated and physical outcomes must be distinguishable. Private holdouts and individual-level human records are not part of the public project.

## What is currently visible

The [demonstrations](DEMONSTRATIONS.md) show product and research directions. They are not scored benchmarks. Historical internal experiments are not automatically public benchmark releases.
