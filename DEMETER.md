# Scentient and Demeter Fragrance Library

**Scentient develops sensory technology and research. Demeter provides the first commercial and physical setting.**

Demeter is the fragrance business, bringing fragrances, domain expertise, physical products and customer experiences. Scentient’s ambition spans agents, interfaces and embodied systems.

## Application direction

**Online:** fragrance exploration through the Demeter website and Personal Perfumer development program.

**In-store:** connecting digital conversation and sensory exploration with physical fragrance encounters.

**Physical creation:** longer-term personalized creation, with early bottling simulations studying individual components of the process.

These directions have different maturity levels. Specific product availability is announced through the relevant product. This description makes no claim about corporate ownership, exclusive rights or a universal live rollout.
