# Development goals

1. Develop useful human sensory experiences through voice, imagery, sound, space and fragrance.
2. Connect the first commercial applications with Demeter online and in-store.
3. Build the multimodal scent-memory episode program with informed participation and clear permitted uses.
4. Advance Prizm’s person-facing agent direction and separate embodied research program.
5. Establish evaluations for personal meaning, uncertainty, correction and cross-model continuity.
6. Extend controlled embodied studies toward integrated tasks, with physical validation reported separately.
7. Publish selected demonstrations and qualified research results.

These are goals, not delivery commitments or availability announcements.
