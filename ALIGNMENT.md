# AI alignment research

## More faithful to the person an agent serves

Scentient investigates whether richer understanding of human sensory experience can help agents interpret intentions, recognize uncertainty, respond to correction, and preserve relevant meaning as models and contexts change.

Our immediate focus is personal alignment and continuity of useful conduct. This contributes concrete questions toward the larger alignment problem; it is not a claim to have solved general AI alignment or established system-wide safety.

## Research questions

- **Meaning and intention:** Can an agent distinguish what a person means from what the system assumes?
- **Correction and continuity:** Can a changed understanding affect later conduct while preserving relevant context that has not changed?
- **Uncertainty and agency:** Can a system ask, abstain, or leave a decision with the person when appropriate, while remaining useful?
- **Cross-model continuity:** What should remain faithful when the model or agent changes?
- **Action and consequence:** Which lessons from experience improve later decisions, and which transfer into new environments?

## Scope of evidence

Human encounters address reported experience and usefulness. Synthetic tasks test defined agent behavior. Embodied experiments test perception and action. Their results are reported separately.

Personalization, agreeable language, engagement, and remembered text are not by themselves evidence of aligned conduct. The relevant question is what the system subsequently does in context.
