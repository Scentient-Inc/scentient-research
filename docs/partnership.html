STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

Demeter & Scentient

Demeter Fragrance Library is Scentient’s strategic partner and our first commercial setting.

Why the fit matters

Demeter’s recognizable everyday scents give us a tangible place to study scent-memory, emotion and affect. A scent can be familiar to many people while carrying a different meaning for each of them. That is the question at the heart of Scentient.

My work with Demeter connects this research to fragrance expertise and real encounters with scent. We can begin with something people can experience, then ask what it means to them.

Two distinct roles

Demeter is the fragrance business and customer experience. Scentient develops the sensory-intelligence research and technology. The partnership gives us a place to begin; our wider ambition reaches personal agents, research environments and embodied AI.

What we want to learn

We want to understand how sensory experience becomes personally meaningful, how memory and affect shape that meaning, and whether the findings help intelligent systems serve people more faithfully.

Using a product does not automatically make someone a research participant. The partnership is an opportunity to do the work, not evidence that the scientific questions have already been answered.

Ross Crames
Founder, Scentient
