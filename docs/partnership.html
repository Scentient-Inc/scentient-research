STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

Our partnership with Demeter Fragrance Library

Demeter Fragrance Library is our strategic partner. Scent and memory have been central to its identity since its 1996 launch.

Why the fit matters

Demeter’s single-note fragrances aim to smell like a photorealistic version of their names: familiar objects, places and experiences. Its craft makes those smells tangible. People bring the memories, feelings and stories that make them personal.

That is why this partnership matters to me. Scentient’s questions begin where a recognizable smell meets a particular person’s life. Demeter gives us a connection to that experience, built through fragrance rather than invented for an AI demonstration.

Two distinct roles

Demeter is the fragrance business and customer experience. Scentient develops the research and technology. Our work starts with scent-memory, autobiographical memory, emotion and affect, and reaches toward intelligent systems that can serve people more faithfully.

What we want to learn

I want to understand how experiences become meaningful, why that meaning persists or changes, and what it can teach us about learning and intelligence. Demeter’s history gives us a reason and a place to begin. The scientific contribution is ours to establish.

Product participation and research participation remain separate.

Ross Crames
Founder, Scentient Inc.
