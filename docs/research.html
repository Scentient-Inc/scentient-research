The questions
behind the products.

01

Human experience.

Does a physical encounter reveal something an equally careful conversation would miss? Our scent-memory program begins here.

02

Personal alignment.

Can an agent use what it learns to act in ways a person endorses, including asking, revising, and stopping?

03

Models of a world.

Can a system predict relevant consequences from observations and actions? A convincing image alone does not answer that question.

04

Useful resources.

Can the resulting environments and evidence help other researchers distinguish better systems from plausible ones?

Research
into experience.

Demeter gives the questions a tangible starting point. Human experiences and controlled environments give them different places to be tested.

Research informs the distinctions our products preserve. Voluntary, appropriately designed studies can then test whether those distinctions improve later outcomes. Product use alone is not research consent.
