The research
agenda.

How does sensory experience become personal meaning, and what can that teach intelligent systems?

We investigate multimodal scent-memory episodes, useful personal agents, continuity across models, and learning in embodied environments. The company’s applications give these questions a concrete place to begin.

What we intend
to establish.

01

Human grounding.

Whether sensory encounters provide useful information about an individual’s later experience beyond conversation and ordinary history.

02

Faithful agents.

Whether an agent can interpret context, respond to correction, preserve relevant meaning, and respect the scope of a person’s intentions.

03

Multimodal learning.

Which relationships across language, imagery, sound, space, and scent are useful for a defined task.

04

Embodied behavior.

Whether an agent can use observations and consequences to improve decisions in controlled environments, with physical transfer tested separately.

These are evaluation goals. No numerical performance claim is published in this website release.
