Scentient and Demeter Fragrance Library have distinct roles in a connected direction.

Scentient.

Sensory technology and research for intelligent systems.

Scentient develops experiences and investigates how sensory context, memory, and personal meaning can become useful to agents and embodied AI. Its ambition extends across interfaces, models, and applications.

Fragrance expertise, physical products, and customer experiences.

Demeter is the first commercial and physical setting for Scentient’s work. A fragrance can be discovered, sampled, worn, compared, and experienced again.

Where the relationship
becomes tangible.

01

Online.

Personal fragrance exploration through the Demeter website and the Personal Perfumer development program. Start from what a person wants to experience, with the fragrance business providing the product setting.

02

In-store.

An application direction connecting conversation and sensory exploration with physical fragrances. The encounter brings the experience beyond a screen.

03

Physical creation.

A longer-term direction toward personalized fragrance creation, with early robotics simulations investigating components of the bottling process.

Application directions and prototypes are in development. Specific availability is announced through the relevant product.

A starting point
with depth.

The value is in the meeting of technology, domain expertise, and real experience.

Demeter grounds the first application in a physical category. Scentient’s research asks what can be learned from those encounters and how useful principles might extend into other intelligent systems.
