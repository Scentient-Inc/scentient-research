COMPANY PRINCIPLES

Three principles

Understand human experience.

Study how sensation, memory and context shape what matters to a person. Recognizing a scent, remembering a place and wanting to wear a fragrance are different experiences.

Learn from the world.

Build intelligence that can learn from encounters and consequences. A convincing account is a starting point; what happens next matters.

Respect human values.

Keep the person’s intentions, choices and authority at the center. A useful system should admit uncertainty, accept correction and respect refusal.

“Predictive ability confers neither permission nor authority.”Scentient · From Sensation to Significance

These principles guide our work. They are commitments, not claims of demonstrated scientific advantage.
