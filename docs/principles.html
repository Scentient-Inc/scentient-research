The person
comes first.

01

Experience has value.

A product should be useful and meaningful to the person experiencing it. Research ambition does not substitute for that value.

02

Personal context deserves boundaries.

People should have agency over how their experiences are represented and used. Trust belongs in the product from the start.

03

Scent is the starting point.

Our first physical application is fragrance. Our long-term category is sensory technology for intelligent systems.

04

Ambition and evidence belong together.

We distinguish product design, simulation, and physical operation. Each step into the world must earn its own evidence.

05

Build the enduring layer.

Interfaces and models will change. Our focus stays on the relationship between intelligent systems and human sensory experience.
