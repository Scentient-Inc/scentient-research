COMPANY PRINCIPLES

Three principles

These are the commitments behind Scentient: understand human experience, learn from the world, and respect human values.

Understand human experience.

Scent-memory, emotion and affect belong at the center of the inquiry. We want to understand what an experience means to someone, in the context of their own life. Recognizing a smell, remembering a person and wanting to wear a fragrance are different things.

Learn from the world.

We use AI to investigate real experiences and what follows from them. Our ideas have to survive contact with evidence. A useful product, a human finding and a better computational method each have to earn their own claim.

Respect human values.

The person has the final word on their experience. A useful system should accept correction, acknowledge uncertainty and respect a refusal. Understanding someone should help us serve their purposes.
