Human experience.
A new foundation for AI.

Building a large-scale body of multimodal scent-memory episodes, grounded in the way people actually experience the world.

One memory.
Many senses.

A scent can carry a room, a voice, a season, a texture, and a feeling. We are building around that richness.

Scentient’s dataset program connects scent and memory with language, imagery, sound, spatial context, and personal interpretation. An episode is a connected human experience, not simply a label attached to a smell.

Uniquely human.
Grounded in participation.

Our thesis is that valuable sensory intelligence grows from direct relationships with people and physical experiences. Demeter gives this work a concrete commercial setting, online and in-store.

01

Personal meaning.

The same scent can mean different things to different people. That individual meaning is part of the intelligence we are building toward.

02

Physical grounding.

Fragrance can be encountered, compared, chosen, and experienced again. Physical participation gives sensory research a grounding beyond descriptions alone.

03

Continuity.

Experiences unfold over time and across interfaces. Our goal is to make that continuity useful to personal agents and, eventually, embodied systems.

Access is a responsibility.

Personal experience is shared in trust. We are building the program around informed participation, clear permitted uses, and respect for the people whose experiences make it possible.

From episodes to embodied intelligence.

Agents need context to help people choose and create. Embodied systems also need to relate that context to environments and physical actions. Our research asks how multimodal human experience can inform that progression, beginning with scent and fragrance creation.

The dataset program is under development. Scale targets, validated modality coverage, and research results will be reported as they are established.
