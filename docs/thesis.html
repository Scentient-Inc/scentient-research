As intelligence scales,
human context matters more.

Our thesis begins with lived experience and reaches toward the systems that will act alongside us.

The next generation of AI will participate in more of the world: helping people choose, create, navigate, and eventually act through physical systems. Its relationship with human experience needs to deepen with it.

The missing sensory layer.

People do not experience life as text alone. Memory carries atmosphere. A familiar smell can belong to a place, a person, or a moment. Taste is personal, and meaning changes with context.

Scentient is building sensory technology for that dimension of intelligence. We want agents and embodied systems to work with richer human context across conversations, environments, and physical experiences.

Why scent comes first.

Scent gives the work a physical starting point. It can be named, explored, combined, delivered, and experienced. Demeter Fragrance Library brings the expertise and commercial foundation to make that starting point concrete.

The first application is fragrance. The company’s ambition is sensory intelligence for a much wider world of intelligent systems.

A foundation built from human experience.

We are building a large-scale multimodal scent-memory dataset: episodes that connect scent with language, imagery, sound, environments, and personal meaning. The opportunity comes from direct human participation and privileged access to physical fragrance experiences, with permission for each intended use.

Our thesis is that this depth of experience can become a distinctive foundation for the AI agent economy and the future embodied AI economy.

Explore the scent-memory program.

A company built through experience.

Our conviction is that enduring value comes from domain expertise, trusted access to real experiences, and knowledge developed through sustained participation. A single generated answer does not create that foundation.

We are bringing those elements together around a focused first market while building toward applications across the agent economy. The strength of that business must be earned through useful products and lasting relationships.

The embodied horizon.

The same ambition extends beyond a screen. Intelligent environments, devices, and robotics will need ways to connect action with human context. Our simulation work explores part of that horizon while the first commercial experience remains grounded in scent.

These are development directions. They do not imply a deployed general-purpose robotics platform or demonstrated human-level sensory understanding.

What we believe.

Personal context should remain personal. Technology should deepen a person’s agency. Experiences should be useful in their own right. And Scentient should remain focused on building the sensory layer, even as its interfaces and underlying models change.
