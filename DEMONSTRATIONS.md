# Selected demonstrations

## Human experience

[Atmospheres and Companion](https://scentient-inc.github.io/scentient-research/experiences.html) show existing human-facing product prototypes and design previews. They illustrate sensory interfaces, not a verified end-to-end human research result.

## Prizm

[Prizm](https://scentient-inc.github.io/scentient-research/prizm.html) presents the person-facing agent direction, existing character design and a spatial-laboratory prototype. Character expression does not depict a measured model emotion or internal state.

## Robotics

[The bottling simulation](https://scentient-inc.github.io/scentient-research/prizm.html#embodied) combines recorded component studies of handling, dosing, filling and closure. It did not produce a physical bottle and is not a manufacturing-service announcement.

A private discussion can distinguish the current implementation from the next research goals: [contact Ross](mailto:ross@scentient.me).
