# Research agenda

Scentient investigates how sensory experience becomes memory, personal meaning, valuation, and behavior, and whether useful computational principles can improve intelligent systems.

## Human grounding

Do actual sensory encounters provide useful information about a person’s later experience beyond capable conversation and ordinary history? Scent and Demeter provide a concrete starting point; they do not limit the company’s long-term scope.

## Multimodal experience

How can language, imagery, sound, space, and scent contribute to expressing an experience? Which relationships are useful for a defined task, and when is a simpler approach sufficient?

## Personal agents and continuity

Can an agent interpret intended meaning, handle uncertainty, respond to corrections, and remain faithful to the person across interactions or model changes? See [alignment research](ALIGNMENT.md).

## Prizm and embodied intelligence

Prizm includes a person-facing agent direction and a separate controlled-environment research program. The latter studies observation, prediction, action, and consequences. Robotics studies are one application. Transfer between synthetic tasks, human benefit, and physical systems remains a separate research question.

## Evidence approach

Compare against capable alternatives. Identify which information each system receives. Report the task, setting, comparison, result and limitations of each released finding. A successful prototype, synthetic test or simulation alone does not establish human benefit or general alignment.

Research releases and bibliography entries will be linked when selected and verified for publication. This release makes no peer-reviewed publication claim.
